Camera, microphone & invitation

Open the three-dot menu to use the Camera selector or Switch camera button to change cameras during a video call. To change your microphone, leave and rejoin. Voice-only participants must rejoin with video to enable a camera. If the host leaves or refreshes, the room ends: start a new room and share its new invitation. Mute and camera on/off controls work during the call.

Anyone with this link can join while space is available. Share it only with your families. The person who started the room must keep it open.

A small note before the big day

This free version connects devices directly, using the public PeerJS signaling service. It has no configured TURN relay: some mobile, office, or restricted networks may not connect. There is no app-imposed call timer, but service availability and your connection can interrupt calls.

Rehearse a long call from all three actual locations. UAE internet-calling restrictions can affect availability; confirm with your provider and keep a tested, approved backup for the ceremony. Video quality depends on your camera, upload speed, and each family’s connection.

Your camera and microphone are requested only when you test or join. The site does not record calls or save chat. PeerJS and STUN providers process connection metadata. This is a private-link room, without account authentication.
